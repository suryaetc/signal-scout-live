Superstocks — Tier 2
What is this page?

This is the Superstocks page — Tier 2 of Signal Scout, a systematic momentum investing system. It lists stocks that have triggered our rules-based entry signal after passing a multi-stage screen. These are not recommendations — they are signals generated entirely by rules, on the record.

The Signals — backtested performance

Each stock is graded by three independent signals. Figures are median outcomes (win rate / median gain / CAGR) from our 15-year systematic backtest.

AlphaReturn engine. How strong and well-structured the stock's longer-term momentum is. Alpha+ → 94% win rate, +76% median, 211% CAGR. Alpha → 85% / +40% / 159%. Alpha-Lite → 76% / +37% / 77%.
VelocitySpeed engine. How fast the stock is moving — flags the quickest compounders, independent of Alpha. Velocity+ → 87% win rate, +59% median, 268% CAGR — the fastest-compounding tier we track. Velocity → 85% / +45% / 133%.
StrengthMarket leadership. Whether the stock, its industry, and its sector are all outperforming the broad market (NSE 500). STRONG++ (all three leading) → 93% win rate, +48% median, 182% CAGR — our most resilient tier in weak markets.
Tiers (conviction) — backtested performance

When more than one signal agrees on the same stock, conviction is higher and the position is sized larger:

▲ ApexAlpha+ with near-max speed (score≥70, RSI≥76, wave3>25) — the return and speed engines maxed together. Our single best-performing cell: 86% win rate, +57% median gain, 220% CAGR.

◆ FocusOur highest-conviction selection list — Apex, Confluence, and every top single-engine tier combined into one action list. 84% win rate, +46% median, 131% CAGR.
Health

Once a stock is held, it gets a quarterly health check — a wellness read on whether it's still performing as expected for how long it's been held. We give positions room to work rather than reacting to daily noise.

PRIMEHealthiest — performing strongly. Add or hold with confidence.
HealthyOn track — hold.
DeterioratingLosing its edge — flagged to exit and redeploy the capital into a fresh leader.

IPO Tab — Ranking Score

Newly listed stocks don't yet have enough monthly history for Alpha, Velocity or Strength, so the IPO tab uses a dedicated Ranking Score instead — RS band, how close the entry was to the breakout level (extension), and whether the stock's industry is leading or lagging the market, combined into one number.

ExtensionHow far above the weekly breakout the entry was. Closer to 0% (validated 74% win rate) is a stronger entry than chasing a name already 8%+ extended.
RS @ EntryRelative strength vs the market, frozen at the entry date so it doesn't drift with hindsight.
IndustryLeading / Weak / Avoid — is the stock's industry itself in an uptrend right now. Combined with a fresh RS≥60 entry, this reaches a validated 92% win rate.
Ranking ScoreThe combined score. Higher is a higher-conviction IPO signal; ✕ EXIT marks a failed breakout — a weekly close back below the fractal.
Exit Signals

A position is held until one of three rule-based exits triggers. Until then, the stop status reads SAFE or WATCH.

SAFEStop intact — within normal range.
WATCHApproaching an exit zone — monitor.
EXHAUSTIONStrength exhaustion exit — momentum has faded and the system steps out of a winner.
DYNAMIC SLDynamic stop — a trailing stop that ratchets up as the trade works, protecting open profit.
FIXED SLFixed (initial) stop — the stop-loss set at entry, capping downside if the trade fails early.

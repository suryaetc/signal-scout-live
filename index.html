Superstocks — Tier 2
What is this page?

This is the Superstocks page — Tier 2 of Signal Scout, a systematic momentum investing system. It lists stocks that have triggered our rules-based entry signal after passing a multi-stage screen. These are not recommendations — they are signals generated entirely by rules, on the record.

The Signals

Each stock is graded by three independent signals. We show what each looks at — not the proprietary formula behind it.

AlphaReturn engine. How strong and well-structured the stock's longer-term momentum is. Higher tiers (Alpha+, Alpha, Alpha-X, Alpha-Lite) reflect rising conviction.
VelocitySpeed engine. How fast the stock is moving — flags the quickest compounders. Independent of Alpha, so it catches movers Alpha alone would miss.
StrengthMarket leadership. Whether the stock, its industry, and its sector are all outperforming the broad market (NSE 500). All three leading = strongest and most resilient when markets are weak.
Tiers (conviction)

When more than one signal agrees on the same stock, conviction is higher and the position is sized larger. The top tiers need multiple signals to line up:

STRONG++Market leadership confirmed at all three levels — stock, industry and sector. The most resilient tier.
CoreBoth the return and the speed engines agree on the same stock.
ConfluenceAn engine signal confirmed by market leadership.
2 of 3Any two of the three signals agreeing.
Health

Once a stock is held, it gets a quarterly health check — a wellness read on whether it's still performing as expected for how long it's been held. We give positions room to work rather than reacting to daily noise.

PRIMEHealthiest — performing strongly. Add or hold with confidence.
HealthyOn track — hold.
DeterioratingLosing its edge — flagged to exit and redeploy the capital into a fresh leader.

Exit Signals

A position is held until one of three rule-based exits triggers. Until then, the stop status reads SAFE or WATCH.

SAFEStop intact — within normal range.
WATCHApproaching an exit zone — monitor.
EXHAUSTIONStrength exhaustion exit — momentum has faded and the system steps out of a winner.
DYNAMIC SLDynamic stop — a trailing stop that ratchets up as the trade works, protecting open profit.
FIXED SLFixed (initial) stop — the stop-loss set at entry, capping downside if the trade fails early.
